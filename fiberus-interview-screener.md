# FiberUs — Week 1 Interview Screener & Guide

## Purpose
Validate the fiber-intake problem among working professionals before committing to any product concept. Not pitching FiberUs — you're a researcher here, not a founder.

---

## 1. Screening criteria (who qualifies)

Recruit 20-30 people. Aim for spread across these, not just people who agree with you:

- Working professional, 22-40 years old
- Desk job / sedentary for 6+ hours/day
- Lives in Mumbai (or major Indian metro)
- Mix of: married/single, cooks at home vs. orders in, gym-goers vs. not

**Disqualify:** anyone already diagnosed with a GI disorder (IBS, Crohn's, etc.) — different problem, different product, skews your data. Note them separately if they come up.

---

## 2. Interview format

- 15-20 minutes, 1-on-1, in person or call
- Don't mention "FiberUs" or your product ideas until the very end (if at all)
- Record with permission, or take notes immediately after
- Goal: get their actual behavior and language, not their opinion of your idea

---

## 3. Warm-up (2 min)

- "Walk me through what you typically eat in a day — breakfast, lunch, snacks, dinner."
- "How would you describe your digestion generally — regular, irregular, fine, don't think about it?"

*(Don't lead. Let them use their own words first.)*

---

## 4. Problem discovery (8-10 min)

- "Do you ever deal with bloating, constipation, irregularity, or digestive discomfort? How often?"
- "When that happens, what do you usually do about it?" *(probe: nothing / home remedy / OTC / isabgol / doctor)*
- "Have you heard of dietary fiber? What do you know about it?" *(tests awareness — don't explain it to them)*
- "Do you think you get enough fiber in your diet? How do you know?"
- "Have you ever tried to deliberately eat more fiber — fruits, vegetables, supplements, anything? What happened — did you stick with it?"
- "What got in the way?" *(probe: taste, convenience, cost, forgot, didn't see results)*
- "Have you used isabgol/psyllium, or any fiber supplement, gummy, or powder? What was that experience like?"

---

## 5. Behavior & context (3-4 min)

- "What snacks do you usually keep at your desk or reach for during the day?"
- "How do you usually discover new health/wellness products — Instagram, friends, pharmacy, Blinkit/Zepto browsing?"
- "If there were a tasty, convenient way to hit your daily fiber — what would it need to look like for you to actually use it daily?" *(let them design it — don't suggest formats)*

---

## 6. Soft willingness-to-pay signal (2 min)

- "If something like that existed and worked well, what would feel like a fair price for a month's supply?" *(anchor-free — don't give a number first)*
- "What would make you *not* buy it, even if it worked?"

---

## 7. Close

- "Is there anything about your digestion or diet I should have asked but didn't?"
- Thank them. Don't reveal FiberUs unless they ask directly what this is for — if they do, keep it vague ("early-stage research on eating habits").

---
